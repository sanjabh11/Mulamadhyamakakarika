**Subject:** New Digital Humanities Resource for Teaching the Mūlamadhyamakakārikā

Dear colleagues,

I am writing to share a new open-access digital humanities tool designed to assist in teaching Nāgārjuna’s *Mūlamadhyamakakārikā* (MMK). 

**Nagarjuna Quantum Reflections** (https://mulamadhyamakarika-quanta.netlify.app/iks-conference) is an interactive web platform that utilizes generative AI and 3D WebGL visualizations to explore the text.

The project maps classical Madhyamaka concepts—specifically *śūnyatā* (emptiness) and *pratītyasamutpāda* (dependent origination)—alongside analogous structures in quantum mechanics (such as superposition and relational entanglement). The pedagogical aim is to provide modern, digital-native learners with an intuitive spatial and interactive framework for engaging with these complex philosophical tenets.

Key features for researchers and educators include:
*   **The ATOM Framework:** Procedural 3D WebGL visualizations that dynamically construct visual metaphors for abstract verses.
*   **Epistemic Transparency:** We have implemented a "Research Mode" and the "RESONANCE metrics system" to explicitly maintain philosophical guardrails against "quantum mysticism," ensuring the AI translates ancient texts accurately.
*   **Multi-Tiered Scaffolding:** Explanations of verses are dynamically adjusted to the user's comprehension level (from introductory to advanced).

This tool is entirely free for academic and research use. We built this as a contemplative-science research tool and warmly welcome feedback from the H-Buddhism community regarding its philosophical accuracy, methodological design, or potential use in undergraduate translation and philosophy seminars.
