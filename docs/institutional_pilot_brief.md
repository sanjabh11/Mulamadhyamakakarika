## Executive Summary

Nagarjuna Quantum Reflections is a Digital Humanities (DH) teaching tool designed for undergraduate and graduate courses in Buddhist philosophy, Indian intellectual history, and comparative philosophy. The platform provides structured, interactive access to Nāgārjuna's *Mūlamadhyamakakārikā* (MMK) — a foundational 2nd-century CE Buddhist philosophical text — with AI-guided scaffolding, 3D visualizations, and tiered pedagogical support.

**Pilot Opportunity:** We are seeking 3-5 institutions to pilot the platform in Fall 2026 courses, with full academic access provided at no cost in exchange for structured feedback.

---

## What the Platform Offers for Your Classroom

### Content Access

- **Complete MMK Corpus:** All 27 chapters, ~448 verses
- **Structured Pedagogy:** Each verse includes:
  - Sanskrit (Devanagari where available, IAST transliteration)
  - English translation (Garfield primary, Siderits cross-reference)
  - Four-layer philosophical explanation (Insight → Madhyamaka → Quantum Parallel → Bridge)
  - Six-question deeperDive FAQ with real-life examples
  - Three-tier quiz system (beginner/intermediate/advanced)

### AI Companion Features

- **Contextual Guidance:** AI assistant trained on MMK-specific system prompts with epistemic guardrails
- **Socratic Dialogue:** Can answer student questions, clarify misconceptions, provide scaffolding
- **Research Mode:** Transparent metadata showing model version, prompt source, safety checks
- **Usage Limits:** Pilot-specific quota adjustments can be provisioned if a formal pilot is arranged

### 3D Visualization

- **ATOM Framework:** WebGL visualizations mapping philosophical concepts to spatial metaphors
- **Example:** Verse 1.13 (Emptiness of Conditions) visualized as "entangled essence-transfer failure"
- **Educational Metadata:** Each animation includes stated learning objectives and caveats

### Assessment Tools

- **Embedded Quizzes:** Three difficulty tiers per verse
- **Progress Tracking:** Chapter completion, streaks, engagement metrics
- **Certificates:** Printable completion certificates for students

## Platform Limitations (Transparent Disclosure)

### Appropriate Use

✅ **Designed for:**
- Introducing MMK to students with no Sanskrit background
- Scaffolding difficult philosophical concepts
- Stimulating discussion on comparative philosophy
- Demonstrating DH methodology (AI + 3D + structured data)

❌ **Not suitable as:**
- Replacement for primary Sanskrit text study
- Authoritative source for academic citation (use Garfield/Siderits)
- Physics education platform (quantum content is philosophical analogy only)
- Replacement for lineage-based instruction, teacher-guided Dharma study, or primary-text commentary traditions

### Known Constraints

1. **Devanagari Coverage:** Expanding but not universal across all verses
2. **AI Limitations:** Gemini responses require verification; not a substitute for instructor expertise
3. **3D Variability:** Earlier chapters have more refined animations
4. **Prototype Telemetry:** Dashboard currently shows illustrative data, not real-time course analytics

## Pedagogical Scenarios

### Scenario A: Flipped Classroom

**Before Class:** Students read verse + philosophy explanation, attempt beginner quiz  
**In Class:** Discuss common misconceptions, use 3D visualization for group analysis  
**After Class:** Advanced quiz for assessment, AI companion for follow-up questions

### Scenario B: Comparative Module

**Week 1-2:** Traditional MMK instruction with primary texts  
**Week 3:** Platform introduction with Research Mode to inspect methodology  
**Week 4:** Student critique of quantum analogies — are the caveats sufficient?

### Scenario C: DH Methods Demonstration

**Focus:** Platform as case study in AI + 3D + structured pedagogy  
**Activities:**
- Compare platform explanation to Garfield translation
- Evaluate RESONANCE scoring methodology
- Assess epistemic guardrails in system prompts
